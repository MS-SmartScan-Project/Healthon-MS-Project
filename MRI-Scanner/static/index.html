Next clinical step

Further evaluation is needed to differentiate MS from SVD.

Tests that help tell MS and SVD apart
  1. Lumbar puncture CSF oligoclonal bands (OCBs) and kappa free light chains (κ-FLC). Positive results support MS; they are not typical of vascular (SVD) lesions.
  2. Contrast-enhanced MRI — brain & spinal cord Spinal cord or enhancing lesions and typical MS locations (corpus callosum, juxtacortical, infratentorial) favour MS. SVD usually spares the spinal cord.
  3. Vascular risk assessment Blood pressure, HbA1c (blood sugar) and lipid profile. Vascular risk factors support SVD.

Diagnostic tests only. This tool does not suggest any treatment.

Technology

From MRI image to clinical insight

A low-cost Raspberry Pi device that brings AI screening for white matter lesions (MS and SVD) to clinics with limited access to neuroradiologists.

  1. 01Capture

    Raspberry Pi Camera Module v2 or direct image upload.

  2. 02Detect

    YOLO26s lesion detector on the grayscale MRI at 640 px, trained to also work on camera photos of printed scans.

  3. 03Focal check

    Regions too large to be a lesion (whole-brain boxes) are ignored, so only focal findings count.

  4. 04Report

    Finding, confidence, flagged regions, and next clinical step — saved to the record.

AI model

YOLO26s trained on MSLesSeg FLAIR slices with expert lesion masks (40 epochs, 640 px, patient-level split). Metrics are on test patients the model never saw.

Device

  • Raspberry Pi 5 (4 GB) with Camera Module v2 and touch display
  • AI analysis runs locally on the device — MRI images are not uploaded to any cloud service
  • Low-cost, portable hardware suited to clinics and screening centers
  • Same software runs on a Windows workstation

Privacy & safety

  • Server accepts connections from this device only (localhost)
  • Required patient identifiers; one medical number per patient
  • Duplicate MRI protection via image fingerprints (SHA-256)
  • CSRF protection, rate limiting, and an audit log of every action
  • Automatic daily database backups (last 7 kept)
